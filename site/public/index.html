Wao: Thai–English dictation, on your iPhone

Wao is a keyboard for iPhone that turns speech into text in any app. Speak Thai with English words mixed in, the way people actually talk, and Wao writes the English words in Latin letters. Speech is transcribed on the device by an open Thai speech model; no audio leaves your phone, and there are no accounts, ads or analytics. Try the beta on TestFlight.
